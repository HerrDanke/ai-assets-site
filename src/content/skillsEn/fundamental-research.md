Defines a deep investment research process starting from business essence, suitable for individual stock research, industry allocation decisions, and long-term position justification.

Five-layer penetration: Business Essence (Duan Yongping perspective), Moat Assessment (Buffett perspective), Financial Health (Graham perspective), Management Evaluation, and Valuation with Margin of Safety.

Output: information richness rating (A/B/C), AI research limitations statement, five-layer analysis summary, operation recommendations.
