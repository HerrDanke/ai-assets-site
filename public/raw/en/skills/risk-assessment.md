Three-layer framework: Layer 1 Market Risk, Layer 2 Stock-Specific Risk, Layer 3 Portfolio-Level Risk.

Risk rating matrix: 1-10 score mapping from very low to very high risk level.

Stress testing: Historical scenario method (2008 Financial Crisis/2015 A-share volatility/2020 COVID) and hypothetical scenario method.
